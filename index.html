ABOUT

WHY DOES A
DENTIST CARE
ABOUT BUSINESS
SYSTEMS?

Because I spent years watching skilled people do skilled work inside systems that were quietly working against them.

I'm a former dental surgeon. No MBA. No engineering background. I now run an education and immigration business that runs on automation, and I work in online commerce management.

Over time I developed a habit:

  • Write down what happens.
  • Where it happens.
  • When it happens.
  • Who does it.
  • What happens next.

Once you document a process, invisible problems become visible.

I became fascinated by process design and early industrial automation — breaking work into repeatable steps, reducing variation, reducing errors, and allowing people to become exceptionally good at what they repeatedly do.

Businesses need the same thinking.
